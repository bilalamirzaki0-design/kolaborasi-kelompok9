How an Old Keyboard Is Transformed into a Wall Clock


Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Old computer keyboards are often discarded when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, these keyboards contain materials that can still be reused. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming old keyboard keys—such as the function keys F1 to F12—into a decorative wall clock. This transformation shows how electronic waste can become a useful and valuable product with aesthetic appeal.


The transformation begins when an unused keyboard is separated into individual components. Its plastic keys, specifically the function keys F1 through F12, are removed from the main keyboard structure and cleaned to remove dust and dirt. At this stage, the original function of the keyboard keys changes; instead of being used for typing, they become decorative materials for a new product.


The cleaned keys are then arranged in a circle on a round clock base to replace the numbers 1 through 12 (key F1 representing number 1, F2 representing number 2, up to F12 at the 12 o'clock position). A clock movement mechanism along with the clock hands (second, minute, and hour hands) is installed in the center of the base. Once the materials are firmly attached and the mechanism is securely fitted, the separate components become one complete wall clock product.


As a result, an old computer keyboard is transformed into a wall clock that has both functional value (as a timekeeper) and technological aesthetic value. This transformation reduces the amount of electronic waste and encourages the reuse of available materials. Therefore, recycling computer components into decorative products is one way to support a more sustainable environment.

How Key and Time Respawned as Click Clock


The concept of unlocking efficiency through scheduling has reached its peak with Click Clock, an innovative time-management system born from the fusion of traditional security keys and temporal data. Historically, managing physical access and tracking operational hours existed as two separate challenges for modern organizations. Click Clock bridges this gap by reimagining the structural purpose of a key, transforming it from a simple metal lock-turner into an intelligent, time-sensitive digital asset. This integration creates a seamless ecosystem where security parameters and time constraints operate as a single unified workflow.


The transformation process began by breaking down the mechanical architecture of traditional keys and merging it with automated time-tracking protocols. Developers extracted the unique identifying cryptographic signatures of physical keys and mapped them onto a dynamic, cloud-based timeline database. Instead of relying on physical ridges to open a door, the system uses digital encryption keys that only activate during pre-programmed windows of time. As temporal data flows through the algorithm, the system continuously updates access permissions, effectively allowing "Time" to shape, mold, and validate the operational state of the "Key."


The final formation of the product materialized as a sleek, cross-platform software suite integrated with compact, IoT-enabled smart locks. The software core features a centralized dashboard where administrators can mint digital keys, assign precise expiration deadlines, and track real-time usage metrics. On the user end, Click Clock operates via an intuitive mobile application that generates encrypted, time-bound QR codes and proximity signals. By blending robust encryption protocols with high-frequency time synchronization, the product emerged as a comprehensive utility that functions simultaneously as an attendance logger and a high-security access controller.


The rollout of Click Clock has fundamentally reshaped workplace operations and significantly reduced corporate security overhead. By eliminating physical brass keys, businesses have seen a drastic reduction in maintenance costs, key replacement fees, and unauthorized entry incidents. Furthermore, the automated synchronization of access logs with payroll networks has streamlined HR workflows, providing error-free tracking of employee hours. Ultimately, Click Clock has set a new standard for modern facility management, proving that when key security and temporal awareness merge, environments become vastly safer and more efficient.

Turning Electronic Waste into a Creative Wall Clock


Electronic waste is a growing problem because people often replace old electronic devices with newer ones. One example is a computer keyboard that is damaged, unused, or no longer needed. Although the keyboard cannot be used for typing anymore, some of its parts can still be useful. The small buttons have different letters, numbers, and symbols that can make an interesting decoration. Instead of throwing the keyboard away, people can reuse its buttons to create a unique wall clock. This project is a simple example of how waste materials can be changed into something useful and attractive.


The process begins by preparing an old keyboard, a round clock base, a clock mechanism, clock hands, adhesive, and simple tools. First, the keyboard is unplugged from the computer and checked to make sure it is safe to handle. Then, the buttons are carefully removed from the keyboard. The buttons are separated from other parts and cleaned with a dry or slightly damp cloth to remove dust and dirt. After that, they are arranged according to their size, shape, or symbols. The clock base is also prepared so that the buttons can be attached neatly.


After all the materials are ready, the clock is assembled step by step. The keyboard buttons are placed around the edge of the round base to represent the numbers on a clock. For example, the function keys from F1 to F12 can be used as the twelve hour markers. Before attaching them, their positions are checked to make sure the distance between each button is balanced. Next, the clock mechanism is placed in the center of the base. The clock hands are attached to the mechanism according to its instructions. Finally, the buttons are fixed with suitable adhesive and left until the adhesive is completely dry. The parts should be checked again to make sure that the hands can move freely and do not touch the buttons.


When the assembly is finished, the old keyboard has become a creative wall clock with a modern and unusual appearance. The clock can be hung on a wall and used to show the time, while its keyboard buttons make it an interesting decoration for a bedroom, classroom, or study area. Each button gives the clock a special design that reminds people of computer technology. This project also teaches creativity, patience, and the importance of reusing materials. By turning an unused keyboard into a new product, people can reduce the amount of electronic waste that is thrown away. Therefore, recycling old keyboard parts is not only useful but also a small action that can help protect the environment.
